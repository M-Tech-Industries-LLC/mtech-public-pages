Afon Browser · Build 0.1.3+29 release notes

Afon Browser — Built Around You

Customize your browser, control how websites open other applications, and browse with clear security and permission choices. Build 29 preserves saved Normal tabs and history across Private Session transitions and improves saved-tab restoration. Android and iOS availability is reported separately on the downloads and testing page.

Privacy Policy Patch Notes Themes Known Issues Downloads & Testing

Your browser, your style

Choose from nine cosmetic themes. Explore previews and redemption guidance in the theme catalog.

Link Protection

Standard is the default. Strict Link Protection is available on Android; it is not available in the iOS / iPadOS release. Insecure HTTP navigation remains blocked.

External applications

Clearer confirmation and authorization management help you control application handoffs. Build 28 reduces repeated prompts and improves Google Maps handoffs and temporary-tab handling.

Private Session

Keep private browsing separate from your normal session. Build 29 preserves saved Normal tabs and history when you enter and leave Private Session. Some incoming external-app links can still leave temporary tabs open; see the active known issues. Private Session does not make you anonymous to websites or network providers.

Website permissions

Build 28 improves website location permission handling across Android, iPhone, and iPad. Permission choices remain subject to your device’s operating-system controls.

Reliable navigation

Build 29 prevents an older restore from overriding newer navigation in the covered restore flow. Restored tabs reopen from saved URLs; unsaved forms, scroll position, media playback, and full Back/Forward history are not promised. Review the release notes for details.
