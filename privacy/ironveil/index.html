Overview

Ironveil is an Android managed runtime and launcher application developed by M-Tech Industries LLC. It is designed to support device launcher functionality, browsing/runtime controls, managed configuration, and operational diagnostics for supported deployments.

Information Handling

Ironveil processes configuration, launcher state, runtime status, policy state, app visibility, and protection-related information locally on the device to provide its app experience and runtime behavior. M-Tech Industries does not sell personal data.

Managed/Enterprise Configuration

Enterprise deployments may apply administrator-managed policies through Android management tools, Managed Google Play, Microsoft Intune, or similar enterprise management platforms. These policies may configure launcher behavior, managed restrictions, app access settings, and runtime controls. Administrator-managed policies may limit what a local user can change.

Diagnostic & Operational Data

Ironveil includes diagnostic systems intended to help understand startup, configuration, policy, and runtime behavior. These diagnostics are designed to avoid storing sensitive browsing contents, search queries, authentication tokens, recovery secrets, personal message contents, clipboard contents, and typed input.

Diagnostics may include non-sensitive operational details such as app version, build mode, device/runtime state, policy source state, configuration reason codes, protection status, launcher visibility summaries, and recent app diagnostic events.

Local Device Processing

Ironveil performs much of its work on-device, including launcher state evaluation, policy interpretation, runtime readiness checks, and local configuration handling. Some data may be stored locally on the device to support setup, preferences, diagnostics, and policy continuity.

Third-Party Services

Some functionality may rely on Android system services, Google Play services, Managed Google Play, enterprise mobility management platforms, or administrator-provided configuration systems. Those third-party services are governed by their own privacy policies and administrator settings.

Security

M-Tech Industries uses reasonable technical and organizational measures for Ironveil development and operation. No software or network-connected system can be guaranteed to be completely secure, and Ironveil does not promise complete prevention of every policy or protection-control failure.

Children's Privacy

Ironveil is not directed toward children under the age of 13. Enterprise or managed deployments should be configured and supervised by the responsible organization or administrator.

Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated periodically as Ironveil evolves, as platform requirements change, or as deployment models are refined. The updated date on this page reflects the latest published version.
