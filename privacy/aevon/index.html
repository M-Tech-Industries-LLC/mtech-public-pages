Overview

Aevon is a browser and managed browsing platform developed by M-Tech Industries LLC.

Information Collection

Aevon may store certain settings and configuration data locally on the device to support browsing preferences, startup behavior, managed configuration, and application functionality.

Browsing Activity

Aevon may process website addresses, navigation actions, and browsing state locally on the device in order to provide browser functionality, security controls, blocked-page recovery, and session restoration features.

Enterprise and Managed Configuration

When deployed in managed or enterprise environments, Aevon may receive configuration settings from enterprise mobility management (EMM/MDM) systems such as Microsoft Intune, Managed Google Play, or similar platforms.

Third-Party Websites

Websites accessed through Aevon are operated by third parties and are subject to their own privacy policies and terms.

Data Sharing

Aevon does not sell personal information. Data handling may vary depending on enterprise deployment configuration and services integrated by administrators.

Security

Reasonable measures are taken to protect locally stored application settings and configuration data.

Children's Privacy

Aevon is not directed toward children under the age of 13.
