Initial Managed Play Readiness Build

  • Status: Controlled testing readiness
  • Product: Ironveil
  • Last updated: May 26, 2026

Overview

This release prepares Ironveil for controlled Android and Managed Google Play testing. It focuses on configuration handling, startup stability, runtime behavior, diagnostics, and release hardening.

Included

  • Managed Play readiness improvements.
  • Managed configuration validation and safe fallback behavior.
  • Startup and runtime stability improvements.
  • Diagnostics and recovery enhancements.
  • Launcher performance and memory optimization work.
  • Release hardening updates.

Testing Notes

  • Validate managed configuration on enrolled physical Android Enterprise devices.
  • Confirm startup, launcher, and runtime behavior after install, reboot, update, policy changes, and policy removal.
  • Review exported diagnostics for operational details without including sensitive browsing content or secrets.

Known Limitations

  • Enterprise deployment behavior depends on Android management platform delivery and configuration.
  • Large blocked-package policy states may require separate performance validation.
  • Some optional device context details depend on Android permission and platform availability.
