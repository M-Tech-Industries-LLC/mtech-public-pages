Current Known Issues

  • Tracker protection uses starter coverage and may not block every tracker.
  • Some websites may still open provider-hosted search or login pages directly.
  • Private browsing reduces local history persistence, but it does not make browsing anonymous.
  • Some platform-specific behaviors may differ between Android and iOS while testing continues.

Recently Fixed

  • Improved blocked-page recovery for everyday navigation.
  • Improved private tab cleanup so closed private tabs do not keep their prior back stack.
  • Updated public links for privacy policy, patch notes, and known issues.

Testing Notes

  • Please test normal browsing, bookmarks, tabs, private sessions, and settings changes.
  • If a legitimate sign-in or redirect is blocked, note the site name and the action you were trying to complete.
  • Aevon does not provide a remote VPN service, location hiding, or complete tracker blocking.
