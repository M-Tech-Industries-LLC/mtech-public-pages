What to Include

  • App name: Ironveil.
  • App version and build number.
  • Device manufacturer, model, and Android version.
  • Whether the device is personally configured or enterprise-managed.
  • Managed configuration source, if applicable, without sharing secrets.
  • Description of the issue and steps to reproduce, if available.
  • Sanitized diagnostics export, if requested by support.

Supported Platform Summary

Ironveil is currently focused on Android devices. Enterprise testing may involve Android Enterprise, Managed Google Play, Microsoft Intune, or similar management platforms depending on the deployment.

Managed Configuration Note

For enterprise deployments, confirm that managed configuration has been assigned and delivered by the management platform before reporting runtime policy behavior as a product issue.

Known Limitations

  • Managed Play and enterprise policy behavior should be validated on enrolled physical devices before production deployment.
  • Large policy inventories may require additional performance validation.
  • Some Android platform permissions and system services must be granted or available for full runtime behavior.
  • SSID display may depend on Android permission and platform behavior.

FAQ

Does Ironveil replace enterprise management?

No. Ironveil may use administrator-managed configuration, but organizations remain responsible for device enrollment, policy assignment, and platform management.

Should support requests include passwords or recovery tokens?

No. Do not send admin passwords, PINs, recovery tokens, signing materials, or managed secrets.
