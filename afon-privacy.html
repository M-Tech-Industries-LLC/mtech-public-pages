Afon Privacy Policy

This page summarizes how Afon handles information in a simple, public format.

Overview

Afon is a browser developed by M-Tech Industries LLC. Afon is designed with privacy-conscious defaults, clear user-facing controls, and straightforward browsing functionality where supported.

Information stored locally

Afon may store settings, preferences, bookmarks, browsing state, and related application data locally on the device to support normal product functionality.

Browsing and third-party sites

Websites opened through Afon are operated by third parties and are subject to their own privacy policies, terms, and data practices.

Feedback and support

Support requests are user initiated. Users should avoid sending sensitive browsing content, credentials, cookies, or private information in support messages.

Data sharing

M-Tech Industries LLC does not sell personal information. Afon public materials do not include advertising claims or analytics-based customer claims.

Contact

Questions can be sent to support@m-techindustries.com.
